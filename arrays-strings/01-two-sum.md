# Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

I used a brute-force approach with two nested loops. Each pair of elements is checked to find two numbers whose sum equals the target. This approach is simple and easy to understand for the problem.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)
